Renewable electricity is associated with wholesale price resilience in sustainable Iberian power markets

Lijing Liu · Elisabeth T. Pereira · Hao Wu

Discover Sustainability · 2026-09-24

Original paper / full textDownload BibTeX

Research overview

This study examines renewable electricity, wholesale prices, gas-price pass-through and Spain–Portugal price dispersion in the Iberian day-ahead market using matched daily samples for 2019–2025.

Key findings

  • Renewable generation is negatively associated with day-ahead prices: −0.987 EUR/MWh per percentage point in the total estimate, and −0.699 EUR/MWh conditional on gas generation.
  • Renewable penetration is associated with weaker within-month gas-price pass-through; the renewable–gas interaction estimate is −0.438.
  • Additional internal Spain–Portugal commercial capacity is associated with narrower hourly absolute price spreads: −2.924 EUR/MWh per additional GW.

Scope and limitations

These estimates are conditional associations, not causal effects. The policy-window comparisons describe temporal heterogeneity and do not establish a causal effect of the Iberian Exception.
