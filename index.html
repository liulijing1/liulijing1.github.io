Conversion Without Redistribution: Generative AI and Academic Authority in International Publishing

This preprint examines whether the early post-ChatGPT period is associated with changes in global academic participation, authorship leadership and recognition. It uses OpenAlex metadata and distinguishes easier conversion of existing research capacity into manuscripts from redistribution of research resources and recognition.
